# BLINKIT_SALES_POWER-BI

🛒 Blinkit Grocery Sales Analysis Dashboard (Power BI)
📌 Project Overview

This project is an interactive Power BI dashboard developed using the Blinkit Grocery Dataset to analyze sales performance across various product categories, outlet types, and outlet locations. The dashboard provides valuable business insights through KPIs and visualizations, helping in better decision-making and understanding customer purchase patterns.

🎯 Project Objectives

Analyze overall sales performance of Blinkit outlets

Identify top-selling product categories

Compare sales based on outlet type and outlet location tier

Understand customer rating trends

Analyze the impact of fat content and item visibility on sales

🛠 Tools & Technologies Used

Power BI Desktop

Excel Dataset

Power Query (Data Cleaning & Transformation)

DAX (Measures and KPIs)

📊 Key KPIs in Dashboard

Average Sales: ₹140.99

Average Rating: 3.92

Average Item Visibility: 0.07

Outlet Count: 10

📈 Insights Derived from the Report
✅ 1. Highest Sales by Item Type

Fruits & Vegetables and Snack Foods contribute the highest sales.

These categories are major revenue drivers in the Blinkit grocery business.

✅ 2. Outlet Type Sales Performance

Supermarket Type outlets generate significantly higher sales compared to grocery stores.

This indicates supermarkets have better product availability and customer demand.

✅ 3. Customer Ratings Across Outlet Types

Supermarket outlets maintain better ratings overall.

The average customer rating across all outlets is approximately 3.92, showing stable customer satisfaction.

✅ 4. Sales Contribution by Outlet Location Tier

Tier 3 outlets contribute a major portion of total sales, showing strong growth and demand in developing areas.

Tier 1 outlets contribute comparatively lower sales.

✅ 5. Fat Content Analysis

Low Fat products generate higher sales than Regular products.

This suggests increasing customer preference toward healthier food options.

✅ 6. Item Visibility Observation

The overall average item visibility is low (0.07).

Even with low visibility, sales remain strong, indicating demand is driven more by product necessity and category preference.

This dashboard provides a clear understanding of Blinkit grocery sales trends and customer preferences. It highlights key sales-driving categories, outlet performance, and consumer patterns, making it useful for business planning and sales optimization.
